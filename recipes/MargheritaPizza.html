Four Cheese Margherita Pizza

How to make an absolutely amazing four cheese margherita pizza that is sure to blow everyone away!

Today we are making a delicious four cheese margherita pizza made from fresh ingredients and full of flavor that is sure to have people reaching for that extra slice! Four cheeses, Roma tomatoes, and basil layer over pre-baked crusts for a tangy, nutty, tasty meal. Reviewers appreciate the easy steps, helping beginner cooks create a successful pizza like a pro.

    ingredients
  • 1/4 cup olive oil
  • 1 tablespoon minced garlic
  • 1/2 teaspoon sea salt
  • 8 Roma tomatoes, sliced
  • 2 (12 inch) pre-baked pizza crusts
  • 8 ounces shredded Mozzarella cheese
  • 4 ounces shredded Fontina cheese
  • 10 fresh basil leaves, washed and dried
  • 1/2 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1/2 cup crumbled feta cheese
